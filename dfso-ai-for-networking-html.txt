  <!-- UNCOVER CONNECTIONS -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <span class="section-tag uncover">Step 1</span>
    </div>
    <h2>Uncover Connections</h2>
    <p class="section-desc">Find out who you should be talking to. Goal: a list of at least 25 people to connect with.</p>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Start with groups you belong to</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt</div>
        <div class="prompt-text" id="p1">
          Here are groups I'm part of. What types of people in these groups are most likely to be connected to <span class="placeholder">[your target — e.g. hiring managers in healthcare, small business owners, HR professionals]</span>?<br><br>
          <span class="placeholder">[Paste your list of groups — church, volunteer org, sports club, professional group, parent group, hobby group, etc.]</span>
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Then follow up: "Which of these groups should I prioritize, and why?"</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here are groups I\'m part of. What types of people in these groups are most likely to be connected to [your target]?\n\n[Paste your list of groups]', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — using your work history</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here are companies I've worked at. What types of people from these workplaces are most likely to be connected to <span class="placeholder">[your target]</span>?<br><br>
          <span class="placeholder">[Paste your list of companies or employers]</span>
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Former colleagues are often your warmest leads.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here are companies I\'ve worked at. What types of people from these workplaces are most likely to be connected to [your target]?\n\n[Paste your list of companies or employers]', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Then find specific names</h3>
      <div class="tip-box">
        <strong>How to do it:</strong> Go to the websites and LinkedIn pages for each group. List the names of people you already know first. Then look for names you don't recognize — board members, event organizers, committee chairs — and add them too.
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- FEED CURATION -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <span class="section-tag feed">Step 2</span>
    </div>
    <h2>Feed Curation</h2>
    <p class="section-desc">Turn your LinkedIn feed into a focused professional tool. Goal: 25–49 people you're actively paying close attention to.</p>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Find who to unfollow</h3>
      <div class="tip-box">
        <strong>Remember:</strong> Unfollowing is NOT disconnecting. You stay connected and can still message them — their posts just stop appearing in your feed.
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — paste your feed posts</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here are recent posts from my LinkedIn feed. Based on the content, are there any posters I should unfollow to keep my feed focused on <span class="placeholder">[your industry / target companies / job function]</span>?<br><br>
          <span class="placeholder">[Paste ~20 posts copied from your LinkedIn feed]</span>
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Copy post text directly from LinkedIn — no need to format it.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here are recent posts from my LinkedIn feed. Based on the content, are there any posters I should unfollow to keep my feed focused on [your industry / target companies / job function]?\n\n[Paste ~20 posts copied from your LinkedIn feed]', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Advanced — if you have 200+ connections</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — upload your LinkedIn CSV</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          These are my LinkedIn connections. What patterns do you see?<br><br>
          <span class="placeholder">[Attach the .csv file you downloaded from LinkedIn]</span>
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">To download: LinkedIn → Me → Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('These are my LinkedIn connections. What patterns do you see?\n\n[Attach the .csv file you downloaded from LinkedIn]', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Follow-up prompt</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          I want to prioritize contacts located in <span class="placeholder">[your region, e.g. the Portland Metro area]</span> who work as <span class="placeholder">[job title]</span> in <span class="placeholder">[industry]</span>. Identify the contacts who do NOT fit these criteria.
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Use the output to unfollow low-priority contacts in bulk.</span>

  <!-- GARDEN NETWORKING -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <span class="section-tag">Garden Networking</span>
    </div>
    <h2>Garden Networking</h2>
    <p class="section-desc">For when you're not urgently job searching. Do this consistently — daily, weekly, or monthly — to stay visible and build relationships over time.</p>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Find posts worth commenting on</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Of these posts, which ones would be worth commenting on if I want to stay visible to people in <span class="placeholder">[my field / my target industry]</span>? For each one, suggest an angle for a genuinely useful comment.<br><br>
          <span class="placeholder">[Paste ~20 posts from your LinkedIn feed]</span>
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">You can reuse the same posts you pasted for Feed Curation.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Of these posts, which ones would be worth commenting on if I want to stay visible to people in [my field / my target industry]? For each one, suggest an angle for a genuinely useful comment.\n\n[Paste ~20 posts from your LinkedIn feed]', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="tip-box">
        <strong>Pro tip for posts with lots of comments:</strong> On a post with hundreds of reactions, your comment may get lost. Try replying to a specific commenter instead — you'll be much more visible to that person. Paste the post AND its comments into AI and ask: "Are any of these comments worth replying to?"
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Draft a comment</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here's a LinkedIn post from <span class="placeholder">[a hiring manager at a company I'd like to work for / a contact I want to stay connected with]</span>. I want to leave a comment that adds value and sounds like me — not generic, not sycophantic. Here's a little about my background: <span class="placeholder">[paste 2–3 sentences about yourself]</span>. Draft two or three options.
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Always edit before posting. The goal is for it to sound like you — because in the final version, it is you.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here\'s a LinkedIn post from [a hiring manager at a company I\'d like to work for / a contact I want to stay connected with]. I want to leave a comment that adds value and sounds like me — not generic, not sycophantic. Here\'s a little about my background: [paste 2–3 sentences about yourself]. Draft two or three options.', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

  <!-- HUNTER NETWORKING -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <span class="section-tag hunter">Hunter Networking</span>
    </div>
    <h2>Hunter Networking</h2>
    <p class="section-desc">For when you need a job now. Do this every weekday during a dedicated time block — focused, direct outreach to specific people.</p>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Find who to reach out to</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — by job target</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          I am seeking work as a <span class="placeholder">[job title]</span> in <span class="placeholder">[industry / location]</span>. Based on my background — <span class="placeholder">[paste 3–4 sentences about your experience]</span> — who are the types of people most likely to either refer me directly to a job opening or connect me with someone who can?
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">This gives you a target profile to search for on LinkedIn.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('I am seeking work as a [job title] in [industry / location]. Based on my background — [paste 3–4 sentences about your experience] — who are the types of people most likely to either refer me directly to a job opening or connect me with someone who can?', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — using your existing contacts</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here are people in my network: <span class="placeholder">[paste list of names and job titles]</span>. Which of them are most likely to know about openings for someone with my background, and which are most likely to know someone who does?
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">A simple name + job title list is enough — no other details needed.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here are people in my network: [paste list of names and job titles]. Which of them are most likely to know about openings for someone with my background, and which are most likely to know someone who does?', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Research a contact before reaching out</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — LinkedIn profile research</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here is the LinkedIn profile of someone I want to reach out to: <span class="placeholder">[paste their profile text]</span>. What do we have in common, and what's the most genuine connection point I could reference in a message?
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">On LinkedIn, click "More" on someone's profile → "Copy LinkedIn URL" then copy their page text.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here is the LinkedIn profile of someone I want to reach out to: [paste their profile text]. What do we have in common, and what\'s the most genuine connection point I could reference in a message?', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — reconnecting after a gap</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          Here is what I know about <span class="placeholder">[name]</span>: <span class="placeholder">[paste what you know about them]</span>. I want to reconnect after <span class="placeholder">[timeframe, e.g. two years]</span>. What's a natural, non-transactional reason to reach out right now?
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Natural reconnection feels like interest in them — not a request.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('Here is what I know about [name]: [paste what you know about them]. I want to reconnect after [timeframe]. What\'s a natural, non-transactional reason to reach out right now?', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="subsection">
      <h3>Draft outreach messages</h3>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — LinkedIn DM</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          I want to send a LinkedIn DM to <span class="placeholder">[name / job title]</span> asking if they know of any openings for someone with my background. I don't want it to sound desperate or transactional. Here's my background: <span class="placeholder">[paste]</span>. Draft two options — one warmer, one more direct.
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Pick the option that sounds most like you, then edit before sending.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('I want to send a LinkedIn DM to [name / job title] asking if they know of any openings for someone with my background. I don\'t want it to sound desperate or transactional. Here\'s my background: [paste]. Draft two options — one warmer, one more direct.', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — warm intro follow-up email</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          I've been referred to <span class="placeholder">[name]</span> by <span class="placeholder">[mutual contact]</span>. Draft a short email introducing myself and asking for a 20-minute call. Here's my background: <span class="placeholder">[paste]</span>. Here's what I know about them: <span class="placeholder">[paste]</span>.
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Mention the mutual contact in the first sentence — it's your best credibility signal.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('I\'ve been referred to [name] by [mutual contact]. Draft a short email introducing myself and asking for a 20-minute call. Here\'s my background: [paste]. Here\'s what I know about them: [paste].', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="prompt-card">
        <div class="prompt-label">Prompt — asking for a warm introduction</div>
        <div class="prompt-text">
          I want to ask <span class="placeholder">[name]</span> to introduce me to <span class="placeholder">[target contact]</span>. We know each other from <span class="placeholder">[context]</span>. Draft a short, easy-to-forward message that makes it simple for them to say yes.
        </div>
        <div class="prompt-footer">
          <span class="prompt-hint">Make it easy — include a one-sentence description of yourself they can use in the intro.</span>
          <button class="copy-btn" onclick="copyPrompt('I want to ask [name] to introduce me to [target contact]. We know each other from [context]. Draft a short, easy-to-forward message that makes it simple for them to say yes.', this)">Copy</button>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

<script>
function copyPrompt(text, btn) {
  navigator.clipboard.writeText(text).then(() => {
    btn.textContent = 'Copied!';
    btn.classList.add('copied');
    setTimeout(() => {
      btn.textContent = 'Copy';
      btn.classList.remove('copied');
    }, 2000);
  }).catch(() => {
    const ta = document.createElement('textarea');
    ta.value = text;
    document.body.appendChild(ta);
    ta.select();
    document.execCommand('copy');
    document.body.removeChild(ta);
    btn.textContent = 'Copied!';
    btn.classList.add('copied');
    setTimeout(() => {
      btn.textContent = 'Copy';
      btn.classList.remove('copied');
    }, 2000);
  });
}
</script>
